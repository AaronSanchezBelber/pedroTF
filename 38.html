Direction for:

XCELIRATE / HIRING PEOPLE

Xcelirate is an unapologetic technology innovator.
They have been market leaders for a decade and continue to set trends in their various niches.
They have opened a new studio, designed by La Granja Design, in Paseo de Gracia. So, we wanted to show the new offices differently to transmit their energy and good work environment through different characters. Each one has different actions and personalities according to the place that they are in.
