Direction for:

PLATO DEL DIA / VIPS

Every day we ask ourselves the same question: What should I eat?
Something tasty? Something practical? Something healthy?
And somehow, making that choice feels like just another task.
At VIPS, we believe it shouldn’t be that complicated.
That’s why we created a dish that has it all: flavor, convenience, and nutrition—all in one.
A complete solution that makes your day easier and takes the weight off deciding what to eat.
Because when everything you need is on a single plate… life becomes simpler, more comfortable, and so much more delicious.
