Direction for:

OLD SPICE / TOMORROWLAND

Every successful DJ needs a bodyguard.
Someone who follows them everywhere, 24/7, ready to protect them from any danger.
SMARTLY RIDICULOUS.
But at Old Spice, we’ve realized something important.
If we’re going to spend the entire summer with our DJs, especially Lost Frequencies, going from festival to festival with their hands constantly in the air, there’s one part of their body that needs some extra protection.
Their armpits.
Because between hours of DJing, jumping, dancing and keeping their arms in the air, their armpits are in for an intense summer.
So this summer, Old Spice is taking DJ security one step further.
We’re not just protecting the DJ. We’re protecting their armpits.
Introducing the newest essential member of their team:
Because even Lost Frequencies needs to keep his armpits cool :D That’s why his very own armpitguard is making sure he stays fresh on his way to @tomorrowland.
Stay fresh, no matter the beat.
