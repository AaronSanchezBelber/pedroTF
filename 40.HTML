Creative Direction / Production for:

YUNG MIKO BRESH / LOOK ALIKE

LOOKALIKE is an event and competition inspired by the cultural and aesthetic phenomenon of Young Miko, one of the most influential figures in the global urban music scene. The concept invited the audience to embody her essence, from her visual style to her stage presence, in a collective experience where the fandom became the protagonist.
The event, held at BRESH with Apolo, was not officially affiliated with the artist, but drew from her universe and influence to create an experience designed around her community.
The climax came with an unexpected twist: Young Miko made a surprise appearance, with no prior announcement or official communication, momentarily blurring the lines between fan and artist.
An unexpected appearance that took the event to another level, turning LOOKALIKE into an unforgettable, one-of-a-kind experience for everyone in attendance.
