Creative Direction / Direction for:

LÒPÉRA NATIONALE DE PARÍS / DEVIALET /

We don’t want to talk about what it does, but about what it makes us feel. Sound here becomes a journey: A force of movement, of transformation. It’s not about dancing to the music — it’s about moving because the sound demands it. Every movement is triggered by an auditory stimulus: the sound doesn’t follow the body it activates it. The protagonist doesn’t dance — he responds. His body becomes an extension of the sound, as if every frequency, beat, vibration, or pause directly affects his nerves, muscles, and balance.
