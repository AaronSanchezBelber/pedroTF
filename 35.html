Production / Direction for:

JUNIOR / SHORTS

A series of short scenes we created a while ago for Junior's acting reel, exploring different emotional and dramatic registers through situations filled with tension, suspense, and intensity.
We wanted to create brief but powerful pieces, giving space to the performance, silences, and subtle expressions to build tension and emotion.
Visually, we went for an intense, cinematic approach, using light, contrast, and framing to heighten the drama of each scene and immerse us in the characters’ emotional states.
The result is a collection of short stories that come together to showcase Junior's versatility and ability to perform across dramatic and suspense-driven roles.
